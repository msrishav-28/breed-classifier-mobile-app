# Screenshot Capture Plan

Capture final screenshots from the signed release candidate or a release
variant with the bundled production model.

## Required Views

1. Home screen with camera and gallery actions visible.
2. Gallery/photo-picker flow selecting a supported breed photo.
3. Results screen showing a high-confidence top prediction, top-3 list,
   quality status, and catalog details.
4. Results screen showing a medium/low-confidence or quality-warning state.
5. History screen with at least two saved classifications.
6. PDF report share/export flow, with the generated PDF opened in a viewer.
7. Camera permission prompt or permission-denied explanation.
8. Dark mode versions of Home, Results, and History.

## Sample Photos

Use held-out or separately licensed real photos for Gir, Hallikar, Murrah,
Sahiwal, and Tharparkar. Do not use training images in public marketing
screenshots unless their license/provenance is suitable for redistribution.

## Device Matrix

- Phone portrait, 1080 x 2400 or similar
- Small/low-end phone, 720 x 1600 or similar
- Optional tablet screenshot if Play listing targets tablets

## Notes

- Show the supported-breed limitation in listing copy, not as an in-app banner.
- Do not show personal data, location, device identifiers, or unrelated
  gallery content.
- Keep the status bar clean and use release branding/icon assets.
