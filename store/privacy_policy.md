# Privacy Policy

Effective date: To be filled before publication.

Breed Classifier is an offline Android app for supported livestock breed
recognition. This policy describes what data the app handles.

## Summary

Breed Classifier does not collect, transmit, sell, or share user data. The app
does not have internet access.

## Permissions

The app requests the camera permission so you can take a livestock photo for
classification. The camera is used only when you open the camera screen.

The app does not request storage permissions. When you choose an existing
photo, Android's system photo picker lets you select only the photo you want
to use.

## Photos And Classification

Photos are processed on your device by a bundled TensorFlow Lite model. Photos
are not uploaded to any server.

Photos selected or captured for classification are stored in app-internal
storage so results and history can be reopened. Other apps cannot access this
storage directly. Photos are deleted when you delete the related history
entry, clear history, or uninstall the app.

## History

Classification history is stored locally on your device in the app database.
You can delete individual records or clear all history in the app. History is
not synced or uploaded.

## Reports

The app can generate a PDF report locally. A report leaves your device only if
you choose to share it through Android's system share sheet.

## Analytics, Advertising, And Identifiers

The app does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking
SDKs. It does not read advertising IDs, device IDs, contacts, location, or
account information.

## Children

The app is not designed to collect information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If future versions add networking, analytics, cloud sync, or any other data
collection, this policy will be updated before that version is published.
